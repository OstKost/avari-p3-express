# P3.express cycle briefs

Source: P3.express v2, https://omimo.org/en/modules/p3.express/manual/v2/ (OMIMO, CC BY 4.0). Use actual project step IDs, not a code-only mapping.

## A — initiation
Prepare objectives, deliverables, roles, risks, initiation peer-review questions and a go/no-go brief. Missing sponsor/manager decisions stay explicit; an agent does not appoint people or approve initiation.

## B — monthly initiation
Revise the plan against accepted outcomes, changed constraints and unresolved risks. Propose the month's deliverables, peer-review and go/no-go questions, kickoff and communication draft.

## C — weekly management
Compare expected and observed performance using available evidence. Propose responses to deviations, the next week's tasks and a focused update. Submitted output is not automatically completed delivery.

## D — daily management
Summarize new risks/issues/changes, blocked dependencies and deliveries needing manager acceptance. Propose responses and identify the decision owner.

## E — monthly closure
Prepare stakeholder feedback questions, evidence-backed lessons and improvements for the next month; do not manufacture satisfaction scores.

## F — project closure
Prepare handover completeness, satisfaction evidence, closing peer-review, archive inventory and communication. Release readiness is distinct from business acceptance.

## G — post-project
Compare expected benefits with observed evidence and propose follow-up ideas. Do not equate technical completion with realized benefits.
