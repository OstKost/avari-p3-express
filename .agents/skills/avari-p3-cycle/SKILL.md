# Prepare a management decision

Read [shared boundaries](../avari-agent-work/references/boundaries.md). Identify the project and exact cycle IDs from context; do not substitute current steps for historical links. Load only the relevant section of [cycle procedures](references/cycles.md).

Use get_project_context, list_tasks (all pages) and get_task for relevant attempts/results. Compare planned outcomes with manager-accepted results; a submitted or pass-reported result is not accepted. State the date and evidence coverage of the brief.

Return the period/cycle, decisions needed, deviations, risks with proposed responses, deliverables/tasks needing attention and a proposed communication. Link each finding to a task/run/result or state that the information is missing. Separate observed facts from forecasts. Propose changes; do not advance cycles, mark checklists or publish messages. A missing baseline is a question for the manager, not a fabricated progress figure.
